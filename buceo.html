Málaga’s Blue Path, site by site

The Senda Azul (Blue Path) is the official dive guide to the Málaga coast: fifteen dives from Manilva to Nerja, each with its depth, level and what you’ll see. Four of them are just off Puerto Banús.

The Galleon: the warship Fernando, 1760

For decades everyone called it “the Galleon” and said it was French. In 2026 the Andalusian Institute of Historical Heritage solved the mystery: it is the Fernando, a Spanish ship of the line built in Ferrol in 1750–51 and lost in 1760.

Its remains lie between eight and ten metres down, 200 metres from the beach: three centuries of history within reach of any diver.

Depth
8–10 m
Level
beginner
You’ll see
frames, ballast and the life on the wreck

Your first dive

Calm, shallow water and me right beside you the whole time. No experience needed, just curiosity.

Get certified

FEDAS · CMAS courses: from One Star Diver (B1E), which lets you dive with a buddy down to 25 metres, to advanced levels and specialties. Recognised worldwide through CMAS.

The Beans Wreck at Punta Carnero

The Dina, a 109-metre freighter that ran aground in an easterly storm in 1983 carrying tinned beans. Today it’s a reef full of life between 8 and 12 metres.
