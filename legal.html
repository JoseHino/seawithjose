Legal notice, bookings and privacy

Who runs this website

Sea With Jose is the personal brand of José María Hinojosa Figueira, based in Marbella (Málaga, Spain). Contact: josemariahf612@gmail.com · WhatsApp +34 665 09 20 90.

What this website does

It presents my sailing trips on the Petit and my other experiences at sea, and lets you book and pay for sailing trips online.

Bookings and payment

Trips are paid in advance by card through Stripe, which processes the payment: this website never sees or stores your card details. Your booking is confirmed when I write to you on WhatsApp or by email. Prices shown are final prices. Extras for private trips are quoted and paid separately.

Cancellations

You can cancel free of charge up to 48 hours before your trip and I’ll refund you in full. If I cancel because of the state of the sea or for safety reasons, you choose another date or a full refund. As this is a leisure activity on a specific date, the 14-day right of withdrawal does not apply (article 103.l of the Spanish consumer protection law).

On board

For safety, the captain decides the route, where to anchor and whether to go out or carry on sailing.

Your data

If you book or write to me, I use your name, contact details and booking details only to organise your trip and meet my tax obligations, and I keep them only as long as required. Stripe processes payment data as an independent controller (stripe.com/privacy). You can ask me at any time to see, correct or delete your data by writing to josemariahf612@gmail.com, and you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).

Cookies

This website uses no cookies and no analytics. While you book, your browser keeps the details of your booking so they can be shown to you when you come back from the payment page. Fonts and images are served from the site itself.

Third parties

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc.), which may record visitors’ IP addresses for security. The WhatsApp, Instagram and webcam links take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.
