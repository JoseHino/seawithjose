Fishing this week

Tuesday 27th is best: fish activity is 4/4, waves reach 0.3 m, and the best window is 13:12–15:12. Bottom fishing and trolling both suit it. Wednesday 28th, from 14:13–16:13, stays almost flat with 3/4 activity. Sunday 1st and Monday 2nd are not worth it: waves, rain and gusts.

When to go out to sea?

Friday 30th is best for sailing, with a 8 km/h SE wind and 0.6 m waves. I would stay in port on Sunday 1st and Monday 2nd because of easterlies, rain, and waves up to 1.7 m and 2.0 m.
