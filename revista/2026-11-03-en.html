Fishing this week

Thursday 6th, from 09:49 to 11:49, is best for bottom fishing and trolling: nearly flat sea and 3/4 fish activity. Friday 7th, from 10:34 to 12:34, also looks good. Solunar theory is guidance, not a guarantee. Saturday 8th and Sunday 9th are not worth it because of rain and rougher water.

When to go out to sea?

Friday 7th is the cleanest day for sailing, although the wind will be light. Stay in port on Tuesday 3rd, Wednesday 4th, Saturday 8th and Sunday 9th because of rain; Sunday brings 48 km/h gusts.
