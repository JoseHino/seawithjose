Two westerly days cooled the water by 0.7 °C

From Thursday 13th to Saturday 15th, water fell 0.7 °C after SW winds of 20 km/h and W winds of 11 km/h. Strong SW winds average a 0.2 °C fall next day, across 172 days. They push surface water offshore, drawing colder water up.

Fishing this week

Friday 20th is the most comfortable day for bottom fishing and trolling: 0.1 m seas, no rain, from 8:22 to 10:22. Monday 23rd brings 4/4 fish activity, solunar theory: guided by the moon, and 0.2 m waves; try 10:53 to 12:53. The tide change near 13:21 may help. Tuesday through Thursday are not worth it because of rain.

When to go out to sea?

Monday 23rd is the best sailing day, with easterlies at 9 km/h and 0.2 m waves, though there will be little wind. Stay in port Tuesday for rain and 36 km/h gusts, and through Thursday for rain.
