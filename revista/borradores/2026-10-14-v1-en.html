Warm 22.4°C water and a diving-led sea logbook

The water has averaged 22.4°C, which is a very welcome temperature for extending the swimming and diving season. This issue looks below the surface in particular, with wrecks, reefs and wildlife worth knowing about.

There is sailing, fishing and ocean science here too, for a quiet read by the marina. If you fancy making the most of this warm spell at sea, I hope to see you aboard or underwater.

With water ranging from 21.4 to 23.6°C, swimming and diving remain comfortable, though a suitable suit makes longer dives more pleasant. Waves stayed below 1.1 m, while the 38 km/h peak gust is a good reason to choose the right shelter and timing; the first quarter and full moon are coming up for anglers who watch the tides.
