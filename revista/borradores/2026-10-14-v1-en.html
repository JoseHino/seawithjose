Sea temperature drops 1.5 °C during a fortnight of easterlies

The standout story this fortnight is below the surface: sea temperature fell by 1.5 °C, three times the average drop for these dates over recent years. Warm days can make it feel like summer is lingering, but the sea is clearly beginning its seasonal turn.

There is plenty else worth watching, from Marbella’s La Bajadilla port plans to a deep bamboo coral forest in the South China Sea. If you would like to make the most of the mild days still ahead, come out on the water with me.

What the data say
Water temperature fell 1.5 °C, three times the usual seasonal drop

Copernicus Marine data show water temperature falling from 23.1 °C to 21.6 °C between 2026-09-29 and 2026-10-13, a drop of 1.5 °C. Over the same dates in the previous three years, the average change was -0.5 °C, making this a sharper cool-down than usual.

The biggest two-day fall was -0.6 °C from 2026-10-05 to 2026-10-07, with E winds of 8 km/h and NE winds of 13 km/h. In the longer record, easterlies tend to be followed by warming and westerlies by cooling, so this fortnight does not show a clear match to the usual pattern.
