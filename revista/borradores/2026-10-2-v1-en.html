Ancient wreck, freed dolphins and an autumn sea

The oldest shipwreck in the world has been found off Turkey, while deep-sea research continues to reveal creatures and habitats we had not known before. Those stories are a good reminder that the sea is still far larger than our charts and routines.

Here in Marbella, the water remains inviting even as autumn settles in. It is a fine time to slow down, check the forecast properly and make the most of a day afloat or underwater.

Average water temperature has stayed very comfortable for swimming and diving, though the right exposure protection now makes the difference on a long dive. Seas have been generally manageable, but the strongest gusts are a reminder to choose the right window; the first quarter is also a useful marker for planning the next fishing trips.
