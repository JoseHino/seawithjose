Easterly winds and rain build from the weekend

We are coming out of a very pleasant week on the water, with little swell and warm days. It is worth making the most of the midweek windows before easterly winds and rain take hold over the weekend.

The last 7 days

The week was dry, with no rain, mainly northerly winds and four days of westerlies. Temperatures reached 25.0 °C on 24 October, while the low was 15.4 °C on the 21st; the strongest gust was a 48 km/h westerly on the 20th.

The week ahead

Wednesday 28th and Friday 30th look like the most comfortable days to head out, with no rain probability and winds of 15 km/h or less. Easterlies set in from Thursday, with a 40% chance of rain, then conditions worsen over the weekend: Sunday 1st has a 95% chance of rain, easterly winds at 25 km/h and gusts of 50 km/h, while Monday 2nd stays at 90%.
