Sea temperature fell 1.8 °C in a week of westerlies

We have had very manageable seas this week, but the water has cooled far more than usual for the time of year. I would make the most of the first half of the week and watch the forecast carefully for the weekend; if you fancy sailing, diving or fishing, I’ll see you in Puerto Banús.

What the data say
Water fell 1.8 °C when it normally rises 0.2 °C

The first drop coincided with a moderate westerly: after 14 km/h from the W on 20 October, water temperature fell 0.5 °C the following day, from 22.5 °C to 22.0 °C. In the record, moderate westerlies of 10–20 km/h are followed by an average fall of 0.13 °C, based on 210 days. The rest of the cooling came with light NW and N winds, so it would be wrong to put the whole change down to one wind pattern.

The week ahead

Tuesday will be overcast, with a 10 km/h SW wind and a 10% chance of rain; Wednesday looks clear, with a 15 km/h SE wind. Easterlies take over from Thursday, at 15 km/h from the E on 29 and 31 October, before a less comfortable spell on 1 and 2 November, with rain chances of 95% and 90%. Sunday is expected to bring 25 km/h easterlies and gusts of 50 km/h.

When to go out to sea?

For sailing, I would choose Friday 30th: it has the best rating, with an 8 km/h SE wind, 0.6 m waves and no rain forecast, although it will be a light-wind day. For bottom fishing, Tuesday 27th is the best option, with 0.3 m seas and traditional solunar activity rated 4/4; the main window is from 13:12 to 15:12, close to the predicted high tide at 15:00. For trolling, Tuesday is also the pick, leaving early between 08:40 and 09:40 while the sea is very flat. I would avoid Sunday 1st and Monday 2nd: easterlies, gusts, likely rain and waves up to 1.7 m and 2.0 m make them poor days afloat.
