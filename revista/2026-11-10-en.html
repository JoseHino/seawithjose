Fishing this week

Saturday 15th is the least poor option for bottom fishing, with 0.6 m seas and 2/4 activity; try 17:06 to 19:06, near sunset. Sunday 16th is fair for trolling, with 13 km/h winds, from 13:07 to 14:07. Solunar theory is a traditional guide. Tuesday to Friday are not worth it because of rain and gusts.

When to go out to sea?

Sunday 16th is the best day for sailing, though still only fair because of rain and 33 km/h gusts. Stay in port from Tuesday to Friday: rain is expected, and Wednesday gusts reach 45 km/h.
